# 6.6 progression foundations — second increment

Depends on the workout-preview increment (PR #3, d500adb). Development candidate;
no merge or production release. The next PR should target the preview branch to
keep its diff focused; retarget main after that increment is approved and merged.

## Behavior
- Strength proposals require the last two distinct-day, comparable exercise
  performances: enough completed sets for the current template, identical loads,
  valid reps and RIR, top-of-range reps and at least one RIR. A load step greater
  than 10% is not proposed automatically. Missing RIR is not interpreted as success.
- Poor current recovery blocks load increases. Repeated failed lower repetition
  targets explain why lighter work should be considered; they do not automatically
  lower weights. More than 21 days without this exercise clears automatic load
  suggestion, including the startSession fallback to old weights.
- Running advice is shown in Train and workout previews with evidence dates. It
  never changes the plan or starts/extends a session. It requires exact recorded
  run kind; generic Garmin imports are not classified using their title or HR.
- A long-run extension needs two comparable completed runs, valid RPE/leg feedback,
  achieved recorded duration targets and no current recovery/leg-load concern.
  Suggestion uses the lower recent duration plus min(5 minutes, floor(5%)).
  The user must evaluate their available time and total weekly load.
- Easy runs are held easy. Interval load is held pending interval-level execution
  evidence. Short sessions with unachieved targets are not labelled fitness loss.

## Heuristics and evidence boundary
The 21/42-day windows, two-session evidence requirement, RIR/RPE cutoffs, 10% duration
comparability and small long-run duration step are conservative product rules for
review, NOT validated individual injury-risk predictions or universal prescriptions.
They are deliberately explainable and deterministic. No language model or external
health-data transmission is added.

Background reviewed: ACSM 2026 position stand, doi:10.1249/MSS.0000000000003897,
https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12965823/ . Its synthesis supports progressive
resistance training but does not validate these exact algorithm thresholds. Do not
claim that frequent exercise changes or periodization are always superior.
